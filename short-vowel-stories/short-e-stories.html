Short E Stories for Beginning Readers

Looking for simple stories that practice the short e sound? Start right here. Short E says /ĕ/ like egg. These tiny stories use beginner-friendly words such as bed, hen, red, ten, and pet.

How to use this page: Pick any story below and read it together. Sound out the short-e words, read the tiny story, and reread it once if it still feels fun. You do not need to start anywhere else first.
