Level 1 — First Words

B is for Bat

Say one sound. Read a few words. Read one tiny story with Cat.

1. Say the Sound2. Read the Words3. Read the Story4. Celebrate
Step 1 · Say the Sound
B

B can say /b/, like bat.

bat
bed
bug
Step 2 · Read a Few Words
batb-a-t
bedb-e-d
bugb-u-g
ballb-a-l-l
boxb-o-x
bagb-a-g
Step 3 · Tiny Story

Cat saw a bat.

The bat had a ball.

The ball was in a box.

A bug sat on the bag.

Cat sat on the bed.

Bye, bat!

Step 4 · Celebrate
⭐

Now you learned B.

That’s a win.

If it still feels fun, read the story again.
