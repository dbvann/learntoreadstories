Level 4 — Long Vowels & Sneaky Letters

The Cool Pool

Read one gentle story with oo. The letters oo can say long oo, like in cool.

1. Say the Sound2. Read the Words3. Read the Story4. Celebrate
Step 1 · Say the Sound
oo

oo can say long oo, like cool.

cool
pool
soon
Step 2 · Read a Few Words
coolc-oo-l
poolp-oo-l
soons-oo-n
moonm-oo-n
Step 3 · Tiny Story

A pool is by the sea.

The pool is cool.

Beach Cat taps the pool.

The moon will come soon.

Beach Cat sits by the pool.

The cool pool shines.

Step 4 · Celebrate
🏊

Now you read words with oo.

That’s a win.

If it still feels fun, read the story again.
