Level 4 — Long Vowels & Sneaky Letters

The Spoon in the Sand

Read one gentle story with oo. The letters oo can say long oo, like in spoon.

1. Say the Sound2. Read the Words3. Read the Story4. Celebrate
Step 1 · Say the Sound
oo

oo can say long oo, like spoon.

spoon
moon
soon
Step 2 · Read a Few Words
spoonsp-oo-n
moonm-oo-n
soons-oo-n
coolc-oo-l
Step 3 · Tiny Story

A spoon is in the sand.

Beach Cat sees the spoon.

The spoon is cool.

The moon is up.

Beach Cat will go soon.

He leaves the spoon.

Step 4 · Celebrate
🥄

Now you read words with oo.

That’s a win.

If it still feels fun, read the story again.
