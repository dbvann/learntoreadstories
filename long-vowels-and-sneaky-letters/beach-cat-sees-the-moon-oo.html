Level 4 — Long Vowels & Sneaky Letters

Beach Cat Sees the Moon

Read one gentle story with oo. The letters oo can say long oo, like in moon.

1. Say the Sound2. Read the Words3. Read the Story4. Celebrate
Step 1 · Say the Sound
oo

oo can say long oo, like moon.

moon
soon
cool
Step 2 · Read a Few Words
moonm-oo-n
soons-oo-n
coolc-oo-l
poolp-oo-l
Step 3 · Tiny Story

Beach Cat sees the moon.

The moon is by the sea.

The sand is cool.

A pool is by the rock.

Beach Cat will sit soon.

The moon is bright.

Step 4 · Celebrate
🌙

Now you read words with oo.

That’s a win.

If it still feels fun, read the story again.
